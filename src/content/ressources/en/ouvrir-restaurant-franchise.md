## Start from the opening date

A backward plan is built in reverse. You set the date of the first service, then place each task before it, starting with those whose timing does not depend on you: the review of an application, a declaration with a deadline, a mandatory training course.

The future franchisee [received the DIP and the draft contract at least 20 days before signing](/en/resources/dip-franchise) (Commercial Code, art. L330-3). The opening plan therefore starts at signing.

For each task, record three things: who owns it (head office or franchisee), what it depends on, and the date it must be finished.

## The premises and the works

A restaurant receives the public. Works that create, fit out or modify an establishment open to the public must be approved before they start (Construction and Housing Code, art. L122-3). The approval checks two things: accessibility and, depending on capacity and type of establishment, fire safety.

Three cases arise:

- The works require a building permit. The permit counts as approval if the competent authority has agreed. If the interior fit-out is not known when applying, a further approval is required before opening to the public.
- The works change the exterior without a permit, for example the shopfront. A prior declaration is filed at the town hall. It is generally reviewed within one month, two months in a protected area.
- A small establishment keeps the same activity. Since law no. 2026-403 of 26 May 2026, the article provides that an establishment under 300 m², fitted with an extinguishing system suited to the fire risk (or located in a railway station) and keeping the same activity, can replace the approval with a declaration of compliance certified by a third party. It is sent before the works, and the authority can object. This scheme awaits a Conseil d'État decree: until it is published, plan for the standard approval.

On the network side, concept plans, the list of required equipment and the works specifications go out before applications are filed. A late plan pushes back the whole chain.

## Hygiene and food safety declarations

A restaurant that prepares or sells food of animal origin declares its establishment before the business starts, to the departmental consumer protection authority (DDPP). It can be done online or by post, and is redone if the operator, address or activity changes.

The Rural and Maritime Fishing Code (art. L233-4) also requires at least one person in each commercial food establishment to have completed specific food hygiene training. It lasts 14 hours, is taught in person, by a provider declared to the regional prefect (order of 12 February 2024, art. 1 and 2). According to the Ministry of Agriculture, the obligation covers traditional restaurants, cafeterias and self-service outlets, and quick-service restaurants. People with three years' experience as a manager or operator in the food sector, or a diploma listed in the order of 18 November 2024 and awarded since 1 January 2006, are exempt. The training has no expiry date.

Schedule this training early. If the franchisee or their manager is not exempt, the course must be completed before the first service.

## The licence to serve alcohol

To serve alcohol with meals, a restaurant declares a restaurant licence or a small restaurant licence (up to 18°) at the town hall, at least 15 days before opening. In Paris and Alsace-Moselle, it is filed at the prefecture. The receipt is proof of the licence.

The person declaring it must have completed the operating permit training: at least 20 hours over at least three days. The permit is valid for 10 years. So plan the training, then the declaration, then the 15 days.

## Hiring and training the team

Each hire requires a pre-employment declaration (DPAE) to the Urssaf. It is made electronically. Failing that, it goes by registered letter, sent no later than the last working day before the hire (Labour Code, art. R1221-5).

Then comes training on the concept: recipes, stations, till, network standards. According to the 22nd Banque Populaire/FFF Annual Franchise Survey, nearly 7 franchisors in 10 favour coaching or mentoring for new franchisees. Plan training for the franchisee before opening, then training for each team member when they join. The franchisee employs the team, so the initial training should also prepare them to train their own crew with the network's materials. Finally, plan for head office to be on site during the first days of trading.

Nor does it replace local rules. The town hall planning department and the department's DDPP confirm what applies to your premises.

## What makes a date slip

An opening rarely slips because of a single task. It slips when one task waits on another and nobody sees it: a plan received late that delays the application, hygiene training booked too close to opening, a licence declaration made 10 days ahead instead of 15. Review the plan with the franchisee every week, and move everything that depends on a date that changes.

_This article describes the rules in force on 10 November 2026. It is not legal advice: local rules and the type of works can change the steps._
